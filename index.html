About Sync Energy

Bridging Nigeria’s Energy and Waste Crisis

Over 85 million people in Nigeria still lack access to reliable electricity, while the country generates more than 16 million tons of waste annually that decomposes, releasing methane and accelerating climate change.

At Sync, We bridge this gap through a smart waste-to-energy ecosystem that converts organic waste into measurable environmental and economic value. We collect organic waste directly from farms, agro-processors, and waste management plants, preventing uncontrolled emissions and converting them into cooking gas, clean energy, and organic fertilizer.

Our initial target market includes 2,000 to 5,000 high-impact users, comprising livestock farmers, agro-processors, industrial operators, residential estates, and underserved communities requiring reliable base-load energy solutions.

Our Technology

From Proof of Concept to IoT-Enabled Intelligence

Following the successful validation of our 25-liter prototype, which achieved stable biogas production over a 16-day testing period, Sync Energy is scaling into its pilot phase with a 1m³ smart biodigester. Equipped with ESP32-based IoT monitoring, the system captures real-time operational data, enabling process optimization, performance analytics, and scalable deployment. This marks the transition from proof-of-concept to intelligent waste-to-energy infrastructure.

Our Service Model

A Scalable Commercial Ecosystem

Following system validation and performance optimization through real-world datasets, Sync Energy transitions into a scalable commercial ecosystem built around clean energy production, decentralized distribution, and infrastructure services.

🔥

Clean Cooking Energy Products

We produce and distribute biogas for household and institutional use, packaged in cylinders and storage bag systems. This provides a direct replacement for firewood, charcoal, and LPG, enabling affordable and clean cooking energy access at the community level.

⚡

Energy-as-a-Service (EaaS)

Sync Energy delivers decentralized energy solutions for mobility and power generation systems, including:

  • Biogas generator conversion kits (diesel and petrol-to-biogas retrofits)
  • Generator optimization and hybrid energy systems
  • Maintenance and technical support services

Achieved through partnerships with local manufacturers and trained field technicians, ensuring scalability and local capacity development.

🌱

Organic Fertilizer Commercialization

The digestate from our biogas process is refined into nutrient-balanced NPK organic fertilizer market-ready for:

  • Farmer cooperatives
  • Agro-processing hubs
  • Regional agricultural supply chains

Supporting soil regeneration and sustainable farming practices.

🏗

Infrastructure & EPC Services

At scale, Sync Energy expands into Energy Generation with Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) services, including:

  • waste-to-energy infrastructure deployment
  • Industrial biogas systems for heat and power generation
  • Advanced gas purification producing compressed biomethane for industrial and commercial fuel systems
  • Modular digester design and installation for off-grid communities and agro-industries
